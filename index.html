KIS (Keep the Internet Safe)

1. Online Pricacy:

a. The biggest rule of online privacy is to NOT share ANY private and personal information online

b. Never re-use the same password

c. Don’t give people you met online any personal information

d. Always reject cookies on websites when possible, they help track you and get your personal information

e. Don’t visit sketchy websites.

f. If something is asking you for a login, always double check the link to make sure it’s not a phishing attack.

g. Don’t put something you wouldn't be okay giving away to a stranger in public.

2. Being a good digital citizen:

a. Think twice before sending something that could be mean

b. If you feel unsafe, tell a trusted adult such as a teacher, guardian, or caretaker

c. If you wouldnt want someone telling that to you, don't say it to them

d. If you wouldnt say it to their face, don't say it at all

3. Digital Footprint:

a. If you wouldnt want your grandma seeing it, don't post it

b. If you were someone who interviews people and saw what you're about to post, would you still hire them?

c. Once it's on the internet, it will be there forever. There is no getting rid of it.





So whenever you are online, make sure to KIS with your friends and other people!
