KIS (Keep the Internet Safe)

Essential tips for digital citizenship, privacy, and online safety.

1. Online Privacy
  • The golden rule: Never share private personal information online.
  • Use unique passwords for every account.
  • Avoid sharing details with people you meet online.
  • Reject non-essential cookies whenever possible to block tracking.
  • Avoid unverified or suspicious websites.
  • Verify links carefully before logging in to prevent phishing attacks.
  • Don't post anything online that you wouldn't share with a stranger in public.
  • If a website doesn't have the 's' in 'https' (like this one), that doesnt mean it's bad, it just means to not send any private information, as it can be intercepted by hackers, but you shouldn't be doing that anyways

2. Being a Good Digital Citizen
  • Think twice before sending messages that could hurt others.
  • If you feel unsafe, inform a trusted adult (teacher, guardian, caretaker).
  • Treat others online the way you want to be treated.
  • If you wouldn't say it in person, don't say it online.

3. Digital Footprint
  • If you wouldn't want a family member to see it, don't post it.
  • Consider if a future employer would approve of your posts.
  • Remember: information shared online can remain accessible forever.

Whenever you are online, make sure to KIS with your friends and community!
